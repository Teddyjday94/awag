About

I build sites the way good work gets done in person: show up, listen, and leave it better than you found it. Your site should load fast, read clearly on a phone, and make people stop scrolling. Most of my clients are owner-operators, and their website is the first handshake.

DesignLayout, type, color, and brand from scratch
BuildHand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
MotionScroll and hover animation that stays smooth
LaunchHosting, domain, Google Business, and analytics

How it
happens

Four steps, usually two to four weeks from first call to launch.

  1. 01

    Call

    We talk through your business, your customers, and the sites you like. You get a fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Design

    You see the homepage first, on desktop and phone, and we adjust it until it feels right.

  3. 03

    Build

    Every page gets built, animated, and tested on real phones. You review a live preview link.

  4. 04

    Launch

    I connect your domain, set up search and analytics, and stick around for changes.
